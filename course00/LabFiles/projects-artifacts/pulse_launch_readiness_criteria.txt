Pulse Launch — Go/No-Go Readiness Criteria

The Launch Program Manager presents these criteria to the Chief Product Officer
at the Day -14 Go/No-Go review. Each criterion must reach the stated threshold;
any criterion below threshold requires either a recovery plan with a named
owner and date, or an executive override.

Category 1: Product Readiness (5 criteria)
- All P0 and P1 bugs from beta closed (threshold: 100% closed)
- Demo environment stable and reflects launch-day functionality (threshold:
  zero crashes in 14 consecutive days)
- Production load testing passes at 3x projected day-one volume
- Telemetry and observability instrumentation deployed and verified
- Rollback procedure tested end-to-end and documented

Category 2: Go-to-Market Readiness (5 criteria)
- Final messaging and positioning approved by CPO
- Sales enablement materials shipped: 1-pager, deck, demo script, ROI calculator
- Top 20 enterprise reps complete certification (threshold: 90% pass rate)
- Demand gen campaigns scheduled and creative approved for Day 0 to Day +30
- Press, analyst briefings, and partner co-marketing calendar locked

Category 3: Customer Success and Support Readiness (4 criteria)
- Onboarding playbook complete with named CSM owner per launch tier
- Support team trained; tier-1 macros and KB articles published
- Beta cohort feedback incorporated; named references signed (threshold: 6
  signed references)
- Customer health monitoring dashboard live for launch cohort

Category 4: Pricing, Billing, and Legal Readiness (4 criteria)
- Pricing operationalized end-to-end: quote-to-cash flow tested
- Standard contracts and order forms reviewed and approved by Legal
- International billing (currencies, taxation) verified for top 5 markets
- Discounting policy and approval matrix communicated to Sales

Category 5: Executive and Cross-Functional Alignment (3 criteria)
- All function leads sign Go/No-Go pre-read 48 hours before review
- Open risks logged with owners and target resolution dates
- Day +30 post-launch review scheduled and on calendars

Total criteria: 21
Threshold for Go: 19 of 21 met OR all unmet criteria covered by approved
recovery plans.
