Cascade Cloud Systems Q3 Fiscal Year 2026 Earnings Press Release Excerpt

October 28, 2026

Cascade Cloud Systems (NASDAQ: CCSY), a leading provider of B2B cloud
infrastructure software, today reported financial results for its third
fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Q3 FY2026 Financial Highlights:
- Total revenue: $187.4 million, up 22% year-over-year
- Subscription revenue: $171.2 million, up 25% year-over-year
- Operating income: $24.1 million, up from $18.3 million in Q3 FY2025
- Net income: $19.6 million, or $0.34 per diluted share
- Cash and cash equivalents: $312 million as of September 30, 2026
- R&D investment: $42 million during the quarter

Q3 FY2026 Customer Metrics:
- Net new customers added: 412
- Total customer count: 8,940 as of September 30, 2026
- Trailing twelve-month gross customer churn: 8.2%
- Net revenue retention rate: 118%

CEO Commentary:
"Our third quarter results reflect continued strong execution against our
strategic priorities. The 22% revenue growth, combined with expanding
operating margins, demonstrates that our investments in platform reliability
and customer success are translating into durable, profitable growth," said
Marcus Chen, Chief Executive Officer of Cascade Cloud Systems. "We remain
focused on three priorities for the remainder of the year: closing our top
50 enterprise pipeline opportunities, completing the integration of our
recent acquisition of DataNexus, and continuing to invest in platform
security."

Forward Guidance for Q4 FY2026:
- Revenue: $198 million to $202 million (representing 18% to 20% growth
  year-over-year)
- Non-GAAP operating margin: approximately 14%

About Cascade Cloud Systems:
Cascade Cloud Systems is headquartered in Seattle, Washington and serves
over 8,900 enterprise customers across 47 countries.
