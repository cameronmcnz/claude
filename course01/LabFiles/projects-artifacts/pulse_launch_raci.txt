Pulse Launch — Cross-Functional RACI

Roles:
- PM: Pulse Product Manager
- ENG: Engineering Lead
- PMM: Product Marketing Manager
- DG: Demand Gen Lead
- SE: Sales Enablement Lead
- VS: VP Sales
- CS: VP Customer Success
- FIN: VP Finance
- LGL: General Counsel
- CPO: Chief Product Officer (Executive Sponsor)

Format: R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed

Pricing finalization
- A: FIN
- R: PMM
- C: VS, CS
- I: CPO, PM

Messaging and positioning
- A: PMM
- R: PM
- C: VS, CS
- I: CPO

Sales enablement materials
- A: SE
- R: PMM
- C: PM
- I: VS

Demo environment and training
- A: PM
- R: ENG, SE
- C: CS
- I: VS

Beta program management
- A: PM
- R: CS
- C: ENG
- I: PMM

Press, analyst, and PR plan
- A: PMM
- R: DG
- C: CPO
- I: PM, VS

Documentation and onboarding content
- A: CS
- R: PMM
- C: SE
- I: PM

Pricing operationalization in billing system
- A: FIN
- R: ENG
- C: PM
- I: CS

Legal review of contracts and terms
- A: LGL
- R: FIN
- C: PM
- I: PMM

Go/No-Go decision
- A: CPO
- R: PM
- C: All function leads
- I: All teams

Launch day execution
- A: PM
- R: All function leads
- C: CPO
- I: Company

Post-launch review at Day +30
- A: PM
- R: All function leads
- C: CPO
- I: Company

Notes:
- PM is Accountable for the overall launch program but Responsible only for
  launch-day execution and the Day +30 post-launch review.
- CPO is the only Accountable role for Go/No-Go.
- Any deliverable not on this list defaults to: Accountable to PM, Responsible
  to the relevant function lead.
