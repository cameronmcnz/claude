Pulse Product Launch Plan Overview

Product: Pulse — an AI-powered analytics platform for marketing teams. Pulse
unifies campaign data across major ad platforms, surfaces performance anomalies
automatically, and recommends budget reallocations.

Company: Meridian Analytics, a 320-employee B2B SaaS company. Annual recurring
revenue (ARR) currently $42M. Pulse is Meridian's first AI-native product,
representing a strategic expansion beyond the company's existing reporting
tools.

Launch Date: Tuesday, May 5 (90 days from kickoff). General availability
worldwide.

Target Customer Segment:
- Mid-market B2B and B2C companies with $50M-$1B in revenue
- Marketing teams of 8-30 people running paid campaigns across 4+ platforms
- Existing Meridian reporting customers as primary expansion targets
- Net-new prospects from competitive replacement deals

Pricing:
- Pulse Standard: $2,400/month (up to 5 connected platforms, 5 user seats)
- Pulse Plus: $4,800/month (unlimited platforms, 15 user seats, predictive
  features)
- Pulse Enterprise: custom pricing, 20+ seats, dedicated CSM

Key Milestones:
- Day -90 (Kickoff): Cross-functional team launches; brand and messaging frozen
- Day -60: Beta with 12 design partners; sales enablement complete
- Day -45: Pricing operationalized in billing; legal review complete
- Day -30: Internal sales training; marketing assets shipped to digital
- Day -14: Final Go/No-Go decision; press embargo lifted at launch
- Day 0: General availability; launch day press, partner content, and demos
- Day +30: First post-launch review; customer cohort analysis

Stakeholders:
- Executive Sponsor: Chief Product Officer
- Program Manager: leads weekly cross-functional sync; owns Go/No-Go prep
- Product: Director of Product, Pulse PM, Pulse engineering lead
- Marketing: VP Marketing, product marketing manager, demand gen lead
- Sales: VP Sales, sales enablement lead, top 5 enterprise reps
- Customer Success: VP CS, Pulse onboarding lead, support manager
- Finance and Legal: VP Finance, General Counsel

Success Metrics (90 days post-launch):
- 200 paying customers across all tiers
- $2.4M in net-new ARR
- 30 named customer references (recorded testimonials)
- Sales-qualified opportunities up 40% vs. prior quarter
- Onboarding time-to-first-value under 14 days
- NPS from launch cohort 40 or higher

Budget: $1.8M total launch spend across product, marketing, sales enablement,
and events.
