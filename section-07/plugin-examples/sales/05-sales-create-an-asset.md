# sales:create-an-asset

**Generate tailored sales assets — decks, one-pagers, and landing pages.**

Takes your product, audience, and sales context and produces a complete, ready-to-use sales asset: a pitch deck outline with slide-by-slide content, a one-pager, an email landing page, or a leave-behind document. Tailored to the specific audience and stage of the sale — not a generic template.

---

## EverClean example

```
sales:create-an-asset

Asset type: one-pager (print and PDF)

Audience: Luxury kitchen designers and architects — trade buyers who will 
  spec EverClean into client projects. They care about design, 
  differentiation, client delight, and whether the product is 
  reliable enough to stake their reputation on.

Product: EverClean — double-wide luxury dishwasher, $3,500–$5,000 MSRP. 
  Two independent cavities: Side A washes, Side B serves as your 
  clean dish cabinet. Eliminates overhead kitchen cabinets entirely. 
  Strong statement piece in open-plan and Scandinavian-influenced kitchens.

Tone: Premium, design-forward, confident — the same voice as the brand
Key message: EverClean is not an appliance upgrade. It is an architectural decision.
CTA: Book a trade showroom visit or request specification sheets

Include: Product dimensions, trade pricing note (available on request), 
  one pull quote placeholder for a designer testimonial, 
  space for a hero product image
```

**What you'll get:** A complete one-pager laid out as structured copy — headline, subheadline, 3–4 feature statements written for a designer audience (not a consumer audience), product spec block, a testimonial placeholder, and a CTA — all in the EverClean brand voice, ready to hand to a designer for layout.

## Useful follow-up prompts

- *"Write a version of this one-pager aimed at luxury home builders rather than designers — same product, different priorities (reliability, delivery, builder margin)."*
- *"Create a 6-slide pitch deck outline for a showroom presentation to a couple who was referred by their architect — they're design-literate but not technical."*
- *"Write the hero landing page copy for a dedicated trade partner sign-up page."*
