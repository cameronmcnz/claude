# sales:competitive-intelligence

**Research competitors and build interactive battlecards.**

Produces structured competitive intelligence — how competitors position themselves, what claims they make, where they are strong, where they are vulnerable, and exactly how to counter their pitch in a live sales conversation. Output is formatted as a battlecard: quick to read, easy to reference mid-call.

---

## EverClean example

```
sales:competitive-intelligence

Our product: EverClean — double-wide luxury dishwasher, $3,500–$5,000. 
  Two independent cavities eliminate the need to empty dishes into 
  overhead cupboards. Strong design statement for open-plan kitchens. 
  Pre-production, limited install base, 2-year warranty, 
  service network still expanding in select markets.

Competitors to research:
- Miele G 7000 Series (primary — most common competitor in luxury segment)
- Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer DD60 (secondary — multi-drawer concept, 
  closest functional analogy to our dual-cavity design)
- Bosch 800 Series (tertiary — most mentioned by homeowners doing research)

Battlecard format: one card per competitor with:
  - How they position themselves
  - Their 3 strongest claims
  - Their 3 biggest weaknesses relative to EverClean
  - The objection we hear most when a prospect mentions them
  - Our best response to that objection (2–3 sentences, conversational)
  - One thing to never say when they come up
```

**What you'll get:** Three one-page battlecards — one per competitor — structured for fast reference during a showroom conversation. Each card includes the competitor's positioning in their own language, the specific objection EverClean is most likely to face (e.g., "But Miele has a 30-year reputation"), and a tested conversational counter that acknowledges the competitor respectfully before pivoting to EverClean's architectural differentiation.

---

## Useful follow-up prompts

- *"Brendan at Stonehaven asked why he should switch from Miele when his clients already trust it — give me the best 3-sentence response."*
- *"Fisher & Paykel's DishDrawer is the closest thing to our dual-cavity concept. What's the clearest way to explain why EverClean is different — not just better?"*
- *"Add a fourth card for Sub-Zero — we're starting to hear them mentioned in the ultra-luxury segment."*
