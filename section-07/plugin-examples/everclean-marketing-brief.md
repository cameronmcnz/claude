## 1. Executive Summary
Every great investment is built on solving a universal human pain point. We aren’t talking about marginal improvements or micro-efficiencies; we are talking about eradicating a daily ritual of suffering. 

That ritual is **emptying the dishwasher**. 

**Project Everclean** introduces a paradigm-shifting, double-wide, dual-cavity appliance that fundamentally redefines the relationship between humans, dinnerware, and cabinetry. By engineering a continuous, alternating loop of washing and storage, Everclean removes the concept of "putting dishes away" from the human experience entirely. 

We are not just selling an appliance. We are selling the reclamation of time, the liberation of cupboard real estate, and a definitive truce in the ongoing war over household chores.

## 2. The Universal Pain Point (The "Dishgruntled" Market)
The traditional kitchen workflow is a broken, linear tragedy:
1. Cook food (creative, rewarding).
2. Eat food (satisfying, joyful).
3. Load dirty dishes into a dark box (menial, but tolerable).
4. **The Roadblock:** The dishwasher finishes running. The dishes are clean. But they are trapped in a state of suspended animation. Someone must manually transport 40+ ceramic and glass objects three feet into an overhead cupboard. 

This final step is where households break down. Clean dishes sit in the machine for days, leading to the catastrophic "sink pile-up." Traditional dishwashers are only functional 50% of the time; the other 50%, they are high-tech roadblocks.

## 3. The Product: How Everclean Works
Everclean is exactly twice as wide as a standard architectural dishwasher, boasting an imposing but beautiful presence under the kitchen island. It features **two independent doors** opening into **two identical, separate dishwashing areas (Side A and Side B)**.

```
       [   E V E R C L E A N   I N F I N I T Y   L O O P   ]
       +-------------------------+-------------------------+
       |         SIDE A          |         SIDE B          |
       |  (Dirty / Loading Box)  |  (Clean / Cabinet Box)  |
       |                         |                         |
       |   [Dirty Dishes In]     |   [Clean Dishes Out]    |
       +-------------------------+-------------------------+
                    |                         |
                    +----<-- THE SWAP --<-----+
```

### The Perpetual Motion Cycle:
* **The Living Cabinet (Side B):** You wake up. Side B is full of pristine, sparkling plates and wine glasses. You do not empty them. You simply pull out a plate, use it, and enjoy your meal.
* **The Catchment Zone (Side A):** Your dirty plate is now loaded directly into Side A. 
* **The Flip:** Over the next 48 hours, Side B slowly empties as dishes are utilized, and Side A slowly fills up with dirty dishes. 
* **The Ignition:** When Side A is perfectly full, you close the door and hit "Wash." 
* **The Metamorphosis:** Side A instantly becomes your new "Clean Cabinet." Side B—now completely empty—becomes your new "Dirty Catchment Zone." 

**Result:** Your dishes are either in active use on the dining table, or they are inside the Everclean. Overhead cupboards are completely obsolete.
