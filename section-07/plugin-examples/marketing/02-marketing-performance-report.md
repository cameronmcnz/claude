# marketing:performance-report

**Build marketing performance reports with metrics and recommendations.**

Paste in raw numbers from any channel — email, paid, social, or events — and it returns a structured report with narrative analysis, trend callouts, and prioritized recommendations. You do not need to format the data first; paste it however it comes out of your analytics tool.

---

## EverClean example

```
marketing:performance-report

Period: EverClean pre-launch campaign, weeks 1–6

Data:
- Landing page visits: 18,400
- Showroom appointment bookings: 312 (1.7% conversion)
- LinkedIn ad impressions: 540,000 | CTR: 0.9% | CPL: $68
- Email teaser list signups: 2,106
- Average time on page: 3m 42s
- Top traffic source: organic (41%), LinkedIn paid (33%), referral from Houzz (18%)
- Bounce rate: 38%

Goal: Drive showroom bookings from affluent homeowners and luxury builders

Audience: CMO and founding team
Format: executive summary with recommendations
```

**What you'll get:** A headline performance summary, channel-by-channel breakdown, what's working and what isn't, and a ranked list of recommendations — written for a leadership audience, not a data analyst.

---

## Useful follow-up prompts

- *"Which channel should we increase spend on for weeks 7–12 based on these numbers?"*
- *"Draft a one-paragraph summary of this report I can paste into our investor update."*
- *"What does a 1.7% showroom booking rate tell us about the quality of our landing page traffic?"*
