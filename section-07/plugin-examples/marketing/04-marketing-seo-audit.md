# marketing:seo-audit

**Run comprehensive SEO audits with keyword and content gap analysis.**

Evaluates a page or topic area for search visibility — covering on-page structure, keyword placement, content depth, and gaps relative to what competitors rank for. Paste the page content or describe the topic and it returns a prioritized fix list.

---

## EverClean example

```
marketing:seo-audit

Content: [paste the EverClean landing page copy]

Target keyword: luxury built-in dishwasher with storage

Audience: Homeowners actively searching for high-end kitchen appliance solutions 
          and architects specifying appliances for custom kitchen builds

Competitors: Miele, Fisher & Paykel, Bosch 800 Series

Focus: both on-page and content gaps
```

**What you'll get:** An on-page audit covering title tag, heading structure, keyword placement, and readability — plus a content gap analysis showing what topics Miele and Fisher & Paykel rank for that EverClean's page doesn't cover yet (e.g., "dishwasher cabinet alternative," "zero-upper-cabinet kitchen," "integrated dishwasher storage").

---

## Useful follow-up prompts

- *"Which of these content gaps should we prioritize first based on search volume and how well they fit our product story?"*
- *"Write a revised H1 and meta description using the audit findings."*
- *"Give me a list of semantic keywords to weave into the existing page copy without rewriting it."*
