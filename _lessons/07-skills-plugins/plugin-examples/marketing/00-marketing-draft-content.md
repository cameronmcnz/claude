# marketing:draft-content

**Draft blog posts, emails, social media, landing pages, and case studies.**

Give it a content type, a topic, and an audience — and it drafts polished, on-brand marketing copy ready to review, refine, or publish. This is the starting point for most marketing content tasks. No strategy or data required.

## EverClean example

```
marketing:draft-content

Content type: landing page hero section
Topic: EverClean — a double-wide dishwasher with two separate cavities that 
       eliminates the need to ever empty dishes into a cupboard
Audience: affluent homeowners, luxury kitchen designers, and high-end home builders
Tone: bold, witty, premium — think Tesla meets Williams-Sonoma
CTA: Book a showroom appointment
```

**What you'll get:** A hero headline, subheadline, 2–3 feature bullets, and a CTA button label — all written in a voice that treats emptying the dishwasher as the absurd daily ritual it actually is.
