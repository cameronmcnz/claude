# marketing:brand-review

**Review content against brand voice and style guides.**

Paste any piece of content alongside a description of your brand voice and it will flag inconsistencies, highlight off-brand phrases, and return either an annotated version with comments or a corrected rewrite. Best used after generating a first draft — before anything goes to a designer or gets published.

---

## EverClean example

```
marketing:brand-review

Content: [paste the EverClean landing page or social copy to review]

Brand voice: Premium, witty, and quietly disruptive. We speak to intelligent 
             people who are tired of being sold incremental upgrades. The tone 
             is confident without being arrogant, humorous without being silly. 
             We never use exclamation marks. We never use the word "innovative." 
             We do not use passive voice. We treat emptying the dishwasher as 
             a genuine domestic injustice worth solving.

Style rules:
- Never say "innovative," "revolutionary," or "game-changing"
- Never use exclamation marks
- Product name is always "EverClean" — never "Everclean" or "EVERCLEAN"
- Price is never mentioned in consumer-facing copy
- Competitors are never named

Output: annotated
```

**What you'll get:** The original content returned with inline comments flagging every violation, plus suggested rewrites for each flagged phrase.

---

## Useful follow-up prompts

- *"Now produce a clean rewritten version with all the flagged issues fixed."*
- *"Check this version of the investor brief against the same brand voice — flag anything that sounds too casual for a financial audience."*
