# sales:draft-outreach

**Research prospects and draft personalized outreach.**

Give it a prospect name, company, and your product context, and it researches the target (public profile, company signals, likely priorities) and drafts a personalised outreach message — email, LinkedIn, or cold call script — that leads with something relevant to them rather than a generic pitch.

---

## EverClean example

```
sales:draft-outreach

Prospect: James Whitfield, Principal, Whitfield & Lowe Architecture
Company: Whitfield & Lowe Architecture — boutique residential architecture 
         firm in Austin, TX. Known for large-format open-plan homes, 
         featured in Dwell and Architectural Digest twice in the past year. 
         Recent projects include a 7,200 sq ft lake house in the Texas Hill 
         Country and a zero-waste spec home in East Austin.

Our product: EverClean — a double-wide luxury dishwasher ($3,500–$5,000) 
             that eliminates the need for overhead kitchen cabinets by 
             combining washing and clean dish storage in one unit. 
             A strong fit for architects designing open-plan, 
             minimalist, or cabinet-free kitchens.

Outreach channel: LinkedIn message
Goal: Get a 20-minute intro call or an invite to visit our Austin showroom
Tone: Peer-to-peer — not a vendor pitch, more like a designer flagging 
      something interesting to another designer
```

**What you'll get:** A personalised LinkedIn message that opens with something specific to James — likely the Dwell feature, the zero-waste spec home, or the Hill Country lake house — then introduces EverClean as a design tool rather than an appliance, and closes with a low-friction ask (coffee, showroom visit, or quick call).

---

## Useful follow-up prompts

- *"Write an email version of the same outreach — slightly longer, with a product image placeholder."*
- *"James hasn't responded after 5 days — write a one-line follow-up that doesn't feel pushy."*
- *"Research three more Austin architects with a similar profile to James Whitfield and draft outreach for each."*
