# sales:pipeline-review

**Analyze pipeline health, prioritize deals, flag risks, and get a weekly action plan.**

Paste your current pipeline — deals, stages, values, last contact dates, and any notes — and it returns a structured pipeline health assessment: which deals to prioritize, which are at risk of going cold or stalling, where coverage is thin, and a ranked weekly action plan with specific next steps per deal.

---

## EverClean example

```
sales:pipeline-review

Week of: July 14, 2025
Sales cycle: Showroom visit → design consultation → quote → signed order

Pipeline:

Deal                          Stage              Value    Last Contact   Notes
----------------------------  -----------------  -------  -------------  ---------------------------
Westlake Build (Hargrove)     Signed order due   $11,400  July 10        Verbal yes, form not returned
Stonehaven Kitchen Design     Trade account      $18,000  July 14        2-unit trial, Aug 15 delivery TBC
Henderson Project (via Stone) Design consult     $9,800   July 14        Patricia speccing for Sept build
Holloway — Showroom Visit     Showroom done      $14,200  July 14        Loved it, sending quote today
Ashworth — Malibu Reno        Quote sent         $9,800   July 6         No response, 8 days
Premier Kitchens Dallas       Spec sheet sent    $27,000  July 3         No response, 11 days
Whitfield & Lowe (Austin)     Outreach sent      $14,200  July 11        LinkedIn, no response yet
Meridian Luxury Homes         Research only      $55,000  Never          Preferred vendor target

Total pipeline value: $159,400
Target for July close: $25,000
```

**What you'll get:** A pipeline health summary — deals ranked by close probability this month, a risk flag on Ashworth and Premier Kitchens (both going cold), the Westlake order called out as the single most urgent item (verbal yes + no signed form), a coverage gap flag if the total closeable pipeline for July is thin vs target, and a day-by-day action plan for the week.

## Useful follow-up prompts

- *"Which three deals should I spend 80% of my time on this week if I can only focus on three?"*
- *"Premier Kitchens Dallas is $27k — write a re-engagement message that opens a conversation without sounding desperate."*
- *"If Westlake falls through, do I have enough pipeline to still hit $25k in July?"*
