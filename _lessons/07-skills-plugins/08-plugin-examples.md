# Plugin Examples to Try

These examples show Claude Plugins in action using a single fictional company — **EverClean** — as the through-line. Every prompt on every page is ready to copy and paste into Claude with the Marketing, Finance, or Sales Plugin installed.

---

## The EverClean Dishwasher

<div class="row align-items-center g-4 mb-4">
  <div class="col-md-7">
    <p>EverClean is a double-wide, dual-cavity luxury dishwasher that solves what its founders call "the last unsolved problem in kitchen design" — the ritual of emptying clean dishes into overhead cupboards.</p>
    <p>The appliance features two completely independent washing cavities: <strong>Side A</strong> washes dirty dishes while <strong>Side B</strong> functions as your clean dish cabinet. When Side B empties as dishes are used, Side A fills with dirties. Hit wash. The sides flip roles. Repeat indefinitely.</p>
    <p>The result: dishes are either on the table being used, or inside the EverClean. Overhead kitchen cabinets become architecturally optional. EverClean is positioned as a premium appliance at <strong>$3,500–$5,000 MSRP</strong>, targeting luxury homeowners, kitchen architects, and high-end home builders.</p>
    <p>The company has just closed a $500k seed round on a convertible note and is moving from functional prototype into tooling and initial production. The examples below follow EverClean across finance, marketing, and sales — from recording the first journal entry to forecasting the first month of revenue.</p>
