# Mermaid Mind Map: AI Risks in Healthcare

A boardroom-friendly mind map of AI risk categories for healthcare executives — patient safety, regulatory, privacy, cybersecurity, and more.

## Starter Prompt

Create a Mermaid mind map showing the major AI risks that healthcare executives need to understand, including patient safety, compliance, data privacy, and cybersecurity.

---

## Expanded Prompt

Create a Mermaid mind map that explains the major areas of AI risk for executives in the healthcare industry.

### Goal

Design a clear, boardroom-friendly mind map that helps healthcare leaders understand the key risks of adopting AI. The audience is professional and non-technical. Present risks clearly and responsibly — not alarmist.

### Center Topic

**AI Risks in Healthcare**

### Required Branches

#### 1. Patient Safety Risks
- Incorrect recommendations
- Missed warnings
- Unsafe automation
- Overreliance on AI
- Lack of human review
- Clinical workflow disruption

#### 2. Regulatory and Compliance Risks
- FDA expectations
- Quality system controls
- Audit readiness
- Documentation gaps
- Validation evidence
- Changing regulations

#### 3. Data Privacy Risks
- Protected health information
- Patient consent
- Data retention
- Third-party tools
- Accidental data exposure
- Cross-border data handling

#### 4. Cybersecurity Risks
- Model misuse
- Prompt injection
- Data leakage
- Vendor vulnerabilities
- Connected device attacks
- Insecure integrations

#### 5. Bias and Fairness Risks
- Biased training data
- Unequal patient outcomes
- Underrepresented populations
- Explainability gaps
- Fairness monitoring
- Ethical review

#### 6. Operational Risks
- Workflow disruption
- Staff adoption issues
- Poor training
- Unclear ownership
- Unreliable outputs
- Vendor dependency

#### 7. Financial and Legal Risks
- Liability exposure
- Failed implementations
- Hidden costs
- Contract risk
- Insurance concerns
- Litigation risk

#### 8. Reputation and Trust Risks
- Patient trust
- Clinician confidence
- Public scrutiny
- Media attention
- Brand damage
- Loss of stakeholder confidence

#### 9. Executive Governance Controls
- AI policy
- Risk ownership
- Approval gates
- Human oversight
- Vendor review
- Monitoring and reporting
- Incident response
- Board-level visibility

### Mermaid Requirements

- Use `mindmap` syntax
- Keep all labels short — no long sentences
- Structure should be clean and scannable when rendered
- Suitable for a boardroom presentation, executive workshop, or governance briefing

### Output Instructions

Return only the Mermaid code block. No explanation before or after.
